Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common inquiries about BlinkAlert

How do I report a technical issue with the app?

For technical issues, you can report the problem directly from within the app by going to Settings > Help & Support > Report a Problem. Alternatively, you can email our support team at support@blinkalert.co.za with details about the issue you're experiencing.

How long does it usually take to get a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent matters related to the app's functionality, our premium support team is available for faster response times.

How can I cancel or modify my subscription?

You can manage your subscription directly through your Google Play account. Go to Google Play Store > Account > Subscriptions > BlinkAlert, and from there you can cancel or modify your subscription. If you need assistance, contact our billing team at billing@blinkalert.co.za.

Can I become a security partner for BlinkAlert?

Yes, we're always looking to expand our security partner network. If you represent a security company or emergency response service, please contact our partnerships team at partners@blinkalert.co.za with information about your company and service area.
